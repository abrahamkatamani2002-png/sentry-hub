What's inside

Security that doesn't get in the way

Everything you need to know who is signing in, prove it's really them, and control what they can touch.

🔐

Salt & pepper

Every password gets its own random salt plus a secret pepper that never touches the database, then it's hashed with scrypt.

✉️

Email verification

After the password, a fresh 6-digit code is emailed. It expires in 10 minutes and works once.

🧭

Role-based access

Admin, staff and user each get their own dashboard, and the server checks every request.

☁️

Encrypted backups

Encrypted snapshots are saved every day, or on demand with one click.
